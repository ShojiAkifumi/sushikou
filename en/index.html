Fresh from Toyosu & Tsukiji

No fuss,
just real Edomae sushi.

At Sushiko, we hand-pick fresh seafood from Toyosu and Tsukiji markets and serve top-quality toppings, including bluefin tuna, at friendly prices.

Enjoy Edomae-style sushi without the formality — whether it's a business dinner, a night out with friends, or a quiet meal on your own. Relax in our cozy little dining room and take your time talking and eating.

We also offer takeout and delivery, so you can enjoy our sushi at home or at the office.
